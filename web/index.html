📡 1. How the Bot Gets Its Data & Syncs Matches

The 5-Stack Coordinator combines real-time Discord session state with official Riot Games Developer API telemetry stored in data/league_stats.json:

🔗 1. Riot Account Linking

Players link one or more Riot IDs (GameName#TAG) via /link-riot. The bot queries Riot's Account-v1 API (/riot/account/v1/accounts/by-riot-id/) to resolve immutable PUUID identifiers and reconciles past match history automatically.

🔄 2. Automated & Manual Match Sync

Every 10 minutes (`auto_match_sync_loop`) or when /sync-matches is triggered, the bot fetches recent match IDs via Match-v5 (/lol/match/v5/matches/by-puuid/) and inspects full post-game telemetry and Riot Challenges data.

🛡️ 3. Squad Verification & Living Threads

Whenever 2 or more linked @LOLers members play on the same team (Blue 100 or Red 200), the match is de-duplicated by match_id, scored by the Holistic Rating Engine, attached to the active session's W-L tally, and synced to the 4 Living Synergy Threads and this dashboard.

🧮 2. Holistic Individual Performance Rating Engine (0–100 Scale)

Unlike raw KDA formulas that unfairly penalize frontline tanks or sacrificial engage supports, rating_engine.py evaluates every player on a 0–100 scale by blending 55% Role Responsibility Weights with 45% Champion Kit Archetype Weights across 6 Performance Pillars:

Blended Pillar Weight = (0.55 × Role_Weight[pillar]) + (0.45 × Archetype_Weight[pillar])
Base Score = Σ (Pillar_Score × Blended_Weight) / Σ (Active_Blended_Weights)
Final Rating = clamp(Base_Score − Excess_Death_Penalty + Outcome_Adjustment, 15.0, 99.5)
⚔️ Pillar 1: Combat & KDA Efficiency

44% KDA ratio (target 3.2) + 34% Kill Participation (target 58% Jng/Sup/Mid, 48% Top/Bot) + 22% Death-rate survival + up to +14 playmaking bonus (Solo Kills & Outnumbered Kills) + +8 carry kill-conversion bonus.

🔥 Pillar 2: Damage & Carry Output

60% Damage Per Minute (benchmarked against kit archetype: 780 Marksman, 760 Mage, 680 Assassin, 650 Splitpusher, 620 Bruiser, 440 Tank, 300 Catcher, 240 Enchanter) + 40% Team Damage Share (target 24% carries).

🐉 Pillar 3: Objective & Structural Pressure

Combines Structural Pressure (Turret Damage + Takedowns ×1500 + Plates ×600) and Monster Control (Objective Dmg + Dragons ×3500 + Barons ×5000 + Heralds ×3000 + Void Grubs ×1200 + Steals ×6000). Weighted 72/28 monster-heavy for Jungle and 72/28 structure-heavy for Top/Splitpushers.

🛡️ Pillar 4: Frontline & Durability

45% Self-Mitigated Damage/min (target 900/m for Tanks/Bruisers/Catchers) + 25% Team Damage Taken Share (target 24% Top/Jng) + 30% Tank Survival Efficiency ((Damage Taken + Mitigated) / Deaths, target 9,500).

✨ Pillar 5: Utility, CC & Enablement

Evaluates CC Score/min (target 1.1/m Catchers/Tanks), Effective Healing & Shielding/min (target 320/m Enchanters), Immobilize & Pick Kills with Allies, and clutch saveAllyFromDeath rescues.

👁️ Pillar 6: Vision, Economy & Lane Lead

Role-benchmarked CS/min (7.2 Bot, 7.0 Mid, 6.8 Top, 5.8 Jng, 1.2 Sup) and Vision Score/min (2.10 Sup, 1.05 Jng), plus bonuses for Laning Gold/XP advantage, +25 CS leads, and Control Wards.

🤝 3. Holistic Pairwise Duo Synergy Formula

The 4 Living Synergy Leaderboards (Bot + Support, Mid + Jungle, Top + Jungle, and Support + Jungle) evaluate duo chemistry on a 0–100 scale with Bayesian sample-size dampening so 1-game flukes don't outrank proven partnerships:

Synergy Score = (0.42 × Bayesian_Win_Rate) + (0.24 × Dampened_Duo_Rating) + (0.14 × Pair_Coordination) + (0.20 × Duo_KDA_Norm)
• Bayesian_Win_Rate = (Raw_WR × min(1.0, Games / 5)) + (50.0 × (1 − min(1.0, Games / 5)))
• Tiers: S Tier (≥ 80.0) | A Tier (≥ 65.0) | B Tier (< 65.0)
